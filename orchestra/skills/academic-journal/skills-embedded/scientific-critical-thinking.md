## Evidence Quality Frameworks
### GRADE (Clinical/Health)
| Level | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| High | Further research unlikely to change confidence |
| Moderate | Further research likely to have important impact |
| Low | Further research very likely to change confidence |
| Very Low | Any estimate is very uncertain |

### Study Design Hierarchy
1. Systematic review / meta-analysis of RCTs
2. Individual RCT
3. Controlled observational study (cohort, case-control)
4. Uncontrolled observational study
5. Mechanistic / in vitro / animal studies
6. Expert opinion / theory

## Evaluation Dimensions
- **Internal validity:** Was the study well-designed for its question?
- **External validity:** Do findings generalize to other populations/contexts?
- **Precision:** How wide are confidence intervals?
- **Consistency:** Do findings align with other studies?
- **Bias risk:** Selection, measurement, confounding, reporting bias?
