# Stage S2.5: Novelty Assessment [Strategist]

**Gate:** Q2.5 (ADVISORY) — novelty audit complete; does not block pipeline.
**Goal:** Systematically assess novelty, contribution type, and scoop risk.
**Needs Composers:** none

## Decisions

### Axis Routing
No axis/passport branching. Runs after Q2 passes (literature review complete).

**Chinese Domestic Routing:** When `venue=chinese-domestic`, scoring expands from 6 to 7 dimensions with the addition of DomesticGap (国内文献覆盖差距). This dimension is critical because Chinese journals have a parallel academic discourse — a paper can appear novel internationally while being well-covered in domestic Chinese literature. CNKI/万方 search results from S2 2B-CNKI feed into this dimension.

### Redundancy Strategy (DP27, Mode B x2)
Novelty scoring uses Mode B x2 redundancy (DP27, S2.5 lines 9-12):
- 2 agents independently score all dimensions (6 for international, 7 for Chinese domestic) on 1-5 scale.
- Standard dimensions: Question, Method, Data, Finding, Theory, Practical
- Chinese domestic adds: **DomesticGap** (国内文献覆盖差距)
- Orchestrator compares:
  - Disagreement >1 on any dimension → flag for review
  - Take average of both scores as final per-dimension score
  - Calculate Overall Novelty Score = average of highest two dimension scores (for domestic: across all 7 dimensions)

### DomesticGap Dimension (Chinese Domestic Only)
**DomesticGap (国内文献覆盖差距):** How well does this paper's contribution stand out within the Chinese domestic literature ecosystem?

Scoring rubric (1-5):
| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 — Breakthrough gap | No Chinese paper addresses this question. The paper bridges a major knowledge gap in domestic literature. Recent CNKI/万方 searches confirm no close competitors. |
| 4 — Wide gap | At most 1-2 Chinese papers tangentially related. Core contribution is absent from domestic discourse. |
| 3 — Moderate gap | Some domestic coverage exists but this paper offers substantially different method/data/conclusion. Position against 3-5 Chinese papers clearly articulated. |
| 2 — Narrow gap | Multiple domestic papers on same topic with similar approaches. Differentiation is incremental (new context, updated data, minor method variation). |
| 1 — No gap / duplication | Domestic literature already well-established on this exact question with similar methodology. Paper reads as a replication or rearrangement of existing Chinese work. |

Assessment inputs (from S2 2B-CNKI):
- CNKI/万方 search results — number and tier of domestic papers on the same topic
- Domestic competing papers table — nearest 3-5 Chinese papers with Delta assessment
- Chinese journal tier analysis — is this contribution appropriate for the target tier?

**Domestic scoop risk:** In addition to standard scoop risk factors, assess:
- Are there recent (last 12 months) Chinese-language publications on the same question?
- Are key authors/groups in China known to be working on this topic?
- Is the data commonly available to Chinese researchers?

### Decision Tree (S2.5, lines 83-92)
```
Novelty Score >= 3.0?
├── YES → Scoop Risk high? → SPRINT mode (arXiv preprint within 2-4 weeks, shorter format)
│   └── Scoop Risk low? → Normal timeline. Target top venue.
└── NO → Novelty Score >= 2.0?
    ├── YES → Proceed with realistic venue expectations.
    └── NO → Novelty Score >= 1.0?
        ├── YES → Proceed ONLY if: thesis requirement / grant deliverable / learning value.
        └── NO (< 1.0) → Strongly reconsider. Pure replication has minimal publication value.
```

**DomesticGate advisory:** When venue=chinese-domestic and DomesticGap <= 2:
- STRONG ADVISORY: Contribution appears incremental in the Chinese ecosystem, even if novel internationally
- Recommend: (a) reframe to emphasize differences from domestic work, or (b) target international journal instead, or (c) acknowledge the limitation and proceed
- This is advisory — does not block pipeline

### SPRINT Mode Trigger (S2.5, line 79)
Enter SPRINT mode IF 2-3 scoop risk factors are "Yes":
1. Hot topic with many groups working on it
2. Close competitor recently published related paper (active program)
3. Data is publicly available (anyone could run analysis)
4. [Chinese domestic] Recent (≤12 months) Chinese-language publication on same question

SPRINT actions: Preprint immediately on arXiv/bioRxiv/SSRN. Consider shorter format (Letter/Brief Report). Submit within 2-4 weeks.

### Contribution Type Classification (S2.5, lines 62-69)
| Novelty Score | Delta Range | Contribution Type | Framing Strategy |
|---------------|-------------|-------------------|------------------|
| >= 4.0 | 4-5 | Breakthrough | "Here we show [field-changing finding]" |
| 3.0 - 3.9 | 3-4 | Substantial Advance | "We extend prior work by showing [specific advance]" |
| 2.0 - 2.9 | 2-3 | Solid Contribution | "We provide new evidence that [finding] using [method/data]" |
| 1.0 - 1.9 | 1-2 | Incremental | "We replicate and extend [prior finding] to [new context]" |
| < 1.0 | 0-1 | Replication | "Our replication [supports/does not support] [prior finding]" |

## Gate

### Q2.5 Gate Checklist (Advisory)
- [ ] All novelty dimensions scored (6 for international, 7 for Chinese domestic)
- [ ] [Chinese domestic] DomesticGap dimension scored with domestic competing papers table
- [ ] Overall Novelty Score calculated
- [ ] 3-5 nearest competing papers identified with Delta magnitude and Journal (for Stage 8.5)
- [ ] [Chinese domestic] 3-5 nearest Chinese competing papers identified from CNKI results
- [ ] Contribution type classified
- [ ] Scoop risk assessed
- [ ] [Chinese domestic] Domestic scoop risk assessed
- [ ] Decision documented

### Gate Failure Route
Q2.5 is ADVISORY — does NOT block pipeline. Even with low score, the decision to proceed is the researcher's. Strongly recommend stopping only if Novelty Score < 1.0 (pure replication). Otherwise, document the assessment and continue.
