# Peer Review (Embedded)

**Source:** `peer-review` v1.0 | **Snapshot:** 2026-06-06
**Pipeline usage:** S9 (Multi-Persona Review, run after paper-audit gate)

## Core Workflow

### Stage 1: Initial Assessment
- What is the central research question/hypothesis?
- What are the main findings and conclusions?
- Is the work scientifically sound and significant?
- Appropriate for the intended venue?
- Any immediate major flaws?

### Stage 2: Section-by-Section Review
For each section (Intro, Methods, Results, Discussion):
- Is the purpose clear?
- Is the methodology appropriate?
- Are claims supported by evidence?
- Are limitations acknowledged?

### Stage 3: Methodology Assessment
- Study design: appropriate for the question?
- Sample size: adequate for the effect size?
- Controls: appropriate and adequate?
- Statistical methods: correctly applied?
- Reproducibility: sufficient detail?

### Stage 4: Reporting Standards
- CONSORT for RCTs
- STROBE for observational studies
- PRISMA for systematic reviews
- STREGA for genetic association studies

### Stage 5: Constructive Feedback Structure
1. Summary of the paper (2-3 sentences)
2. Major concerns (numbered, with justification)
3. Minor concerns (numbered)
4. Suggestions for improvement
